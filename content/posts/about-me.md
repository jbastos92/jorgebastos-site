I’m Jorge Bastos, a full stack developer from Brazil with about ten years of experience.

My most recent work was on [e.CETESB](https://e.cetesb.sp.gov.br/), the online services platform of CETESB, São Paulo’s state environmental agency. I helped build new features and modules such as Reclamações (complaints), Infrações (violations), and Inspeções (inspections). I built some of them end to end, frontend and backend, and they were the last modules added to the platform.

These days I’m strengthening my system design skills and studying AI engineering. I write notes here about what I’m learning. I’m also open to working with international teams.
